Intelligent AgriDiagnose was recognized at the ASEAN Digital Awards 2026 for its innovative approach to agricultural technology and its significant impact on empowering farmers across Southeast Asia. The project demonstrates how AI can be made accessible to underserved communities through familiar platforms.

Project Overview

Intelligent AgriDiagnose is a revolutionary AI-powered system that enables farmers to diagnose corn diseases through simple photo uploads via popular messaging platforms—Line, WhatsApp, and Facebook Messenger. The system leverages advanced Deep Learning technology (ResNet architecture) to provide accurate, real-time disease classification without requiring farmers to download any specialized applications.

Key Innovation

Unlike traditional agricultural diagnosis systems that require specialized apps or equipment, Intelligent AgriDiagnose integrates seamlessly into platforms farmers already use daily. This accessibility-first approach removes technical barriers and empowers farmers with immediate, scientifically validated disease identification capabilities.

Technical Implementation

AI Architecture

  • Deep Learning Model: ResNet (Residual Neural Network) architecture optimized for agricultural disease classification
  • Computer Vision: Advanced image processing techniques for accurate disease detection from farmer-submitted photos
  • Real-time Processing: Efficient inference pipeline enabling instant diagnosis through messaging platforms

Platform Integration

  • Line Messenger: Widely used in Thailand and across Southeast Asia
  • WhatsApp: Global reach with extensive farmer user base
  • Facebook Messenger: Complementary platform for broader accessibility

Technical Highlights

  • Scientifically validated disease classification models
  • Cloud-based infrastructure for scalable deployment
  • Multi-language support for diverse farming communities
  • Secure data handling and privacy protection

Collaborating Institutions

This project represents a successful collaboration between academic research and governmental agricultural expertise:

  • Kasetsart University: Thailand's leading agricultural university, providing research expertise and validation
  • Thailand National Corn Research Center: Official research center contributing domain knowledge and field data for model training and validation
  • Fusion Solution Co., Ltd.: Technology partner responsible for AI development and system integration

The partnership ensures that the technology is both scientifically rigorous and practically relevant to Thai farmers' needs.

Impact & Accessibility

Empowering Farmers

Intelligent AgriDiagnose democratizes access to advanced agricultural technology by:

  • Eliminating the need for smartphone app downloads or specialized equipment
  • Providing instant disease diagnosis through familiar messaging platforms
  • Offering scientifically validated recommendations for disease management
  • Supporting early disease detection to prevent crop losses

Addressing Real Challenges

  • Accessibility: Works on basic smartphones with messaging apps—no high-end devices required
  • Ease of Use: Simple photo-based interface requires minimal technical knowledge
  • Cost-Effective: Free diagnosis tool accessible to farmers regardless of economic status
  • Scalability: Cloud infrastructure enables nationwide and regional deployment

Future Development

Building on the success at ASEAN Digital Awards 2026, future development plans include:

  • Expansion to additional crop types beyond corn (rice, cassava, vegetables)
  • Integration of treatment recommendations and agricultural best practices
  • Regional deployment across ASEAN countries with localized models
  • Collaboration with more agricultural institutions for enhanced model accuracy
  • Development of farmer community features for knowledge sharing
  • Integration with agricultural supply chain platforms for comprehensive farm management

The ultimate vision is to create a comprehensive AI-powered agricultural support ecosystem accessible to all farmers across Southeast Asia.
